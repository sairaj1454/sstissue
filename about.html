About Us

Learn more about Sri Sahasra and our commitment to quality

Our Story

Sri Sahasra Enterprises is a leading manufacturer and distributor of high-quality tissue products in the region. Our journey began with a simple mission: to provide premium tissue solutions that meet the diverse needs of our customers.

Over the years, we have expanded our product range and improved our manufacturing processes to deliver tissue products that stand out for their quality, softness, and durability. Today, we serve a wide range of clients, from restaurants and hotels to hospitals and households.

At Sri Sahasra, we believe in combining traditional craftsmanship with modern technology to create products that exceed expectations. Our dedicated team works tirelessly to ensure that every product that leaves our facility meets our high standards of excellence.

Our Mission & Vision

Guided by our core values, we strive to make a positive impact in the tissue industry.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality tissue products that enhance comfort and hygiene in everyday life. We are committed to using sustainable practices and innovative technologies to deliver products that meet the evolving needs of our customers while minimizing our environmental footprint.

Our Vision

To be the preferred choice for tissue products globally, recognized for our unwavering commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction. We aspire to lead the industry in sustainable practices and set new standards for excellence in tissue manufacturing.

Our Quality Standards

At Sri Sahasra, quality is not just a promise—it's a practice embedded in every aspect of our operations. We adhere to stringent quality control measures throughout our manufacturing process to ensure that our products meet the highest standards of excellence.

Our quality assurance team conducts rigorous testing at every stage of production, from raw material selection to the final packaging. We use advanced technology and follow international quality standards to deliver products that are safe, hygienic, and reliable.
